Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

OnlineChalo.in (OnlineChalo, we, us or our), a unit of Shree Services in Mumbai, uses information to respond to enquiries and provide website, e-commerce and web-application development and related services. This policy covers our own website, sales enquiries and client communications. Client-operated websites and applications have their own privacy arrangements.

1. Information we receive

  • Website enquiries: the contact form asks for your name and phone number, with service interest, a message and budget range. Our quote landing page asks for your name, phone number, business type and service interest. Only fields marked as required must be completed.
  • Conversations: information you share by WhatsApp, email or phone, including your contact details and project requirements. An email address is received when you email us; it is not a field on the current website enquiry forms.
  • Projects and services: business details, requirements, project discovery answers, communications, proposals, invoices and billing or payment-status records relevant to an engagement. Please do not send card passwords, one-time passwords or unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Website use: hosting and active Google advertising tools may process IP addresses, browser or device details, page visits, referrals and interactions.

Demo website forms prepare an enquiry to OnlineChalo in WhatsApp. They do not submit an appointment to a real clinic or professional. Review the prepared message before sending it and avoid including medical or other sensitive details.

2. How we use information

We use relevant details to answer enquiries, arrange consultations, prepare quotations, understand project scope, deliver and support services, communicate about billing, maintain business records and operate our website. Existing advertising tools help measure visits and enquiries.

Submitting an enquiry is not automatic permission for unrelated promotional messages. Any separate marketing permission should be requested separately, and you can ask us to stop promotional communications.

3. Facebook and Instagram enquiries

If we offer a Meta instant form in a future Facebook or Instagram campaign and you submit it, we will receive the contact and project details included in that form. We will use them to respond about the service you requested, discuss requirements and follow up on that enquiry. The fields and any separate marketing choice will be shown in the form.

Meta processes activity on its platforms under its own Privacy Policy. We do not sell lead-form information.

4. Forms, hosting and other providers

  • Vercel: hosts this website and handles technical requests and associated hosting information. With your analytics permission, the website-design page loads its existing Vercel Web Analytics integration, which can process page-view, referral and device information if the service is configured. Successful collection in the provider dashboard has not been verified. See Vercel's Privacy Notice and Web Analytics privacy information.
  • Web3Forms and email: website contact and quote forms submit through Web3Forms for delivery to our enquiry inbox, hello@onlinechalo.in. Web3Forms processes submissions and technical or anti-spam information under its Privacy Policy. Its storage and retention practices are separate from copies in our inbox.
  • Google: Google Ads measurement loads only with your advertising permission. Google Maps on Contact and About loads only with external-media permission or when you choose “Enable Google Maps”. Maps can load additional Google resources, including map images and Google Fonts. Those services can share technical information with Google. The main website's fonts are served locally. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Project discovery: our project discovery workspace saves answers in your browser's local storage and sends draft or submitted answers to a configured Google Apps Script endpoint for project scoping. Local storage is specific to that workspace; clearing it removes the local copy, not copies already sent.
  • WhatsApp and external links: following a WhatsApp link opens that service; we receive a message when you send it. External websites and platforms handle information under their own privacy terms.

Information may also be disclosed where required by law or needed to address a dispute or security issue. Providers may process information outside India under their own terms and applicable requirements. We do not sell your personal contact details for another business's independent marketing.

5. Cookies and measurement

Optional advertising, analytics and external media are off until you allow them. “Accept All” allows all three categories, “Reject Optional” leaves them off, and “Manage Preferences” lets you choose separately. Use the “Cookie Preferences” button in any page footer to change your choices. Forms, WhatsApp/email/phone links and the project workspace remain available when optional services are rejected.

  • Advertising: when allowed, the existing Google Ads tag can measure visits and conversions, including quote-page WhatsApp/call interactions and the thank-you page, and support personalised advertising and audiences. Google can use cookies or similar identifiers. We use basic Google Consent Mode v2: Google advertising tags are not loaded, and no denied-consent measurement pings are sent, before advertising permission.
  • Analytics: the existing Vercel Web Analytics integration is loaded on the website-design page only when this category is allowed. Its documented measurement uses no third-party cookies; dashboard collection remains unverified. No new Google Analytics 4 or GTM installation is included.
  • External media: Google Maps receives technical information only when you enable maps. An ordinary external link opens the provider's own service when you follow it.
  • Necessary storage: we save your category choices, notice version and save/expiry times in this browser's local storage under onlinechalo:consent:v1 for 180 days. No identifier is added to that preference record. The project discovery workspace separately stores project drafts as described above. Hosting, form delivery and provider anti-spam handling are needed for the services you request; they do not depend on optional tracking permission.

Removing permission stops future optional loading and may reload the page to stop an already active tracking script. Please finish or copy any unsent message first. We remove known first-party tracking cookies where browser access permits. We cannot delete cookies held on another provider's domain or information already received by that provider. You can also manage cookies and clear site data in your browser; clearing the saved preference means you will be asked again. If browser storage is unavailable, preferences apply to the current page and may not be remembered.

Meta Pixel is not installed or enabled, including after “Accept All”. A disabled consent integration point is reserved for a later reviewed installation. We will update this notice before enabling it or adding other tracking tools. Cookie choices are separate from permission for marketing messages and do not erase enquiries or client records already submitted.

6. Retention and security

We have no fixed published retention schedule. How long a record is needed depends on the enquiry, project and support relationship, business record needs, disputes and applicable legal obligations. You can contact us to ask about a particular record or request deletion; some records may need to be retained. Providers apply their own retention terms.

This website and its configured form endpoints use HTTPS to protect information in transit. No internet transmission or storage method is completely secure. Please share only the information needed for your enquiry or project.

We may need enough information to verify that a request relates to you. We will review requests in light of applicable requirements and any records we need to retain.
