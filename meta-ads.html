Questions before you start.

Clear answers help define the right scope.

Is this social-media content management?

No. This is paid campaign management. Organic content creation, ongoing posting and general social-media account management are not included.

Can leads go to WhatsApp or an instant form?

These are campaign routes to discuss during setup. We agree the fields, privacy notice, follow-up ownership and how lead receipt will be tested before launch.

Is ad creative production included?

Creative assets, production requirements and responsibility are agreed separately in the scope. Do not assume photography, video or ongoing content production is included.

Will Meta Pixel be installed automatically?

No. Meta Pixel remains disabled on this website. Any future installation requires a separate tracking, consent and disclosure review.

What results can you promise?

We do not promise a fixed lead volume, sales result or return. Agree the objective, tests and lead-quality measures before launching.
