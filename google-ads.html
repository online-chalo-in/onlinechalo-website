Questions before you start.

Clear answers help define the right scope.

Is ad spend included in the management fee?

No. Media spend is separate. The management scope, fee, budget and responsibilities should be agreed before campaigns start.

Do you guarantee leads or return on ad spend?

No. Results depend on the offer, competition, budget, landing page and follow-up, among other factors. We agree what to measure and use the evidence to guide changes.

Will you change my tracking setup?

Tracking changes are discussed separately, after reviewing the existing setup, privacy disclosures and consent requirements.

Can you improve the landing page too?

We can scope website or landing-page development alongside campaign work. It is important to agree the deliverables and fees rather than assume a rebuild is included.
