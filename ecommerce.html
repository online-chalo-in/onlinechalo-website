Questions before you start.

Clear answers help define the right scope.

Can you build a catalogue without online checkout?

Yes. A catalogue or product showcase can help customers browse and enquire. A transactional store adds checkout and order-handling requirements. We scope the right approach for your business.

Which e-commerce platform will you use?

The platform is a project decision, based on your requirements, existing systems and maintenance needs. We do not assume a particular platform before reviewing the scope.

Can you connect payments and shipping?

We assess the payment and shipping requirements during discovery. Provider eligibility, account setup, technical feasibility and recurring charges need to be confirmed before an integration is included.

What does maintenance include?

Maintenance can cover agreed updates, hosting and support. The actual tasks, responsibilities, fees and support arrangements are written into the project scope.

Who owns the website and code?

Ownership, access, handover and any third-party licences need to be set out in the project agreement. We discuss these details before development rather than assume the same arrangement fits every project.
